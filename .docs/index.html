C. Formal Movement Rules, One-Shot Goal Semantics & Reproducible Code

Shared 2D Problem & One-Shot Goal-Locking Rules

Discrete 4-Connected Grid: Time advances in discrete steps t = 0, 1, …. At each step, an agent either waits on its current cell or moves to one of the 4 cardinal neighbors.

One-Shot Stay-at-Goal Occupancy: Once an agent reaches its assigned goal cell, it transitions to the finished state and remains stationary at its goal for the remainder of the episode. Occupied goals block other agents from entering them. An episode is recorded as Solved only when all agents reach and remain at their goals before the horizon expires.

MAPPO Training vs. Evaluation Time

Centralized Training, Decentralized Execution (CTDE): The shared policy actor observes a 9×9 egocentric 4-channel window plus goal vector (327 features), while the centralized critic observes the global joint state during PPO updates.

Separate Timing Accounting: Search planners (Prioritized A* and CBS) plan online per instance, whereas MAPPO invests compute during training and executes forward policy rollouts at evaluation time. Both metrics are reported distinctly in all tables and exports.
